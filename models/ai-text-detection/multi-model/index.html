step 3 — the stealth duel

Greedy vs sampled, same prompt

Greedy decoding always picks the most likely next token — maximally “typical” machine text. Sampling spreads probability mass, which should make text harder to catch. The GPT-2 report measured exactly this effect (nucleus sampling was hardest to detect).

Why this is the interesting part

This is the whole cat-and-mouse game of synthetic-text detection, compressed into one tab and two local models. The watchdog usually wins on its home turf — GPT-2 writing reads like GPT-2 writing — but the duel shows the arms race: generation settings move the score. That's why OpenAI paired the detector's release with the caveat that ~95% “is not high enough accuracy for standalone detection”, and why any real product treats detection as one signal among many. Seeing the generator and the judge side by side makes that concrete in a way no blog post can.
