Basics: machine or human, live

The simplest thing the detector does: read a passage and decide whether it reads machine-written or human-written, with a confidence. Type in the box and the verdict updates a moment after you pause. Then hit “Score the pairs” to put genuine GPT-2 output next to human writing and watch the model separate them.
