Practical: zero-shot tagging

Instead of hand-labeling a media library, ask CLIP to score every asset against the labels you care about. This demo runs the catalog against three tags at once and shows each image's real similarity — the core of auto-tagging pipelines, done privately on-device.

Each card shows the image with its three tag scores; the highest-scoring tag is highlighted. This is how a zero-shot tagger works: no training, just embeddings and a threshold.
