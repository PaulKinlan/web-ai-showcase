Basics: search the catalog

The fundamental move: type a description, get the catalog ranked. No labels, no training — the query is embedded and compared to each image's embedding with cosine similarity, live on your device.

Scores are cosine similarities (−1…1); the top card is the model's best match. Try "a dog" vs "a wheel" to see how the ranking changes. Head to Practical for zero-shot tagging, or Wild for absurd queries.
