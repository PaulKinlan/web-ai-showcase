Wild · counterfactual stress test

How much surface noise changes the verdict?

Keep the underlying message, add symbols or machine-readable fragments, and compare two real model runs. This makes false-positive risk tangible instead of hiding it behind one label.

A changed score is not lost meaning

This model was trained for an English gibberish taxonomy. Code, URLs, identifiers, creative spelling, multilingual text, and assistive shorthand can remain meaningful to people while moving the model toward “noise” or “mild gibberish.” It is not a universal spam or safety detector.

What to look for

The model is sensitive to token patterns, not human intent. Compare labels across strength settings. The code + URL perturbation is especially useful when assessing support forms, developer tools, and logs; a high gibberish score there is evidence to segment traffic, not to blame the author.
