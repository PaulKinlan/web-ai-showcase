Practical · batch quality review

Triage input quality without uploading the text

Score up to twelve lines in a dedicated worker, then apply a visible, adjustable policy. “Review” means ask a human or ask for clarification—not delete, block, or punish.

Do not turn this queue into an automatic ban list

The model is English-domain and can false-positive on short answers, dialect, names, non-English text, code, URLs, and noisy but meaningful messages. It does not detect universal spam, abuse, fraud, or safety risk.

A safer product pattern

  1. Measure false positives on representative, consented traffic.
  2. Keep all four scores and policy version for debugging.
  3. Let people edit, explain, or override.
  4. Sample accepted and reviewed items for drift; never infer intent from “gibberish.”
