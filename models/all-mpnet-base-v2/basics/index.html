Do two sentences mean the same thing?

The fundamental capability, felt directly: MPNet turns each sentence into a 768-d unit vector, and cosine similarity — the dot product of those vectors — is one number from −1 to 1 that says how close in meaning they are. This cosine is an uncalibrated, model-specific relatedness score (higher = more similar): it is not a calibrated probability or a universal threshold, so read the number rather than trusting a fixed band. Edit either line, compare it again, and watch the real score move.
