Recast the story

Every story is one casting decision away from a different universe. The model finds the actual named entities — the people, organisations and places — without you marking anything up. Then you recast each one: put yourself on the throne, move the capital to your street, hand the company to your cat. Type your replacements and rebuild the text with its grammar untouched and its names gloriously wrong.
