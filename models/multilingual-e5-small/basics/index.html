Basics — how alike are these two texts?

The whole idea in one dial: two texts go in, one number comes back — the cosine similarity of their meanings, from −1 (opposite) through 0 (unrelated) to 1 (the same meaning). Try a translation pair, a paraphrase, or two unrelated sentences — in any mix of languages.
