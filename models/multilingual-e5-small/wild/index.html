Wild — the model can't tell languages apart, only meanings

Twelve sentences: four topics (food, weather, sport, money), each said in three different languages. We hand all twelve to the model as one batch, then for every sentence ask a simple question of the real similarity matrix: which other sentence is your nearest neighbour? If the space is truly shared, every sentence should link to a same-topic sentence in a different language — forming cross-language topic chains with zero keywords in common. Press run and inspect the chains.
