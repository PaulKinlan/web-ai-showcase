Basics — one photo, 21 classes

The fundamental capability, plainly: you give the model a photo, it gives back a label for every pixel. This page shows the mask, which classes were found, how much of the frame each covers, and how confident the model was — nothing more.

What am I looking at?

Every coloured region is a set of pixels the model assigned the same class. The legend is sorted by coverage: on the hiking photo "person" usually dominates; on the dog photo "dog" does. The confidence is the mean of the model's per-pixel confidence over that region — high confidence on large clear subjects, lower on thin structures like the bicycle frame.
