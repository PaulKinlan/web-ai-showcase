Wild — class spotlight

A photo becomes a set of semantic layers. Segment it once, then click a class in the legend — person, dog, bicycle — and everything that isn't that class sinks into darkness. Click again to bring the world back. It's a strangely compelling way to see a scene the way the model does.

How it works

One pass of the model produces the category mask; the spotlight is pure rendering — the mask pixels of the chosen class keep the photo, everything else is dimmed to near-black. Because the mask is per-pixel, the spotlight hugs the outline of the thing you picked: a bicycle frame, a dog's ears, the crowd in the market.
