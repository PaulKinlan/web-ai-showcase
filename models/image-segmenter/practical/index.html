How it works

The model's category mask says which pixels are person. The page keeps exactly those pixels of the photo and makes the rest transparent (the checkerboard shows through). "Download PNG" exports that transparency — the coloured backdrop is only a preview. Because DeepLab labels every person in frame, group shots cut out everyone at once.
