Practical: a local product cut-out studio

Select the sneaker—or upload your own product photo—then tune the real foreground threshold and download transparency. The original never leaves the device, and the export is made from the worker-composited MagicTouch confidence mask.

What a product could ship

  • Background removal inside a marketplace listing form, even offline after caching.
  • A privacy-first sticker or catalogue-image maker with no image retention policy to explain.
  • Human review of the confidence, point, coverage, and crop before committing the edit.

MagicTouch is optimized for interaction, not pixel-perfect professional matting. Thin laces and close adjacent objects may be missed or merged; the visible threshold is a real recovery control, not a quality guarantee.
