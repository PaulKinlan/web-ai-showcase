Basics: one point becomes a pixel mask

Click the dog, cat, person, shoe, or bicycle. MagicTouch encodes your point as the fourth input channel and returns background/foreground confidence for every pixel. Compare a point on the subject with a corner point: the coverage, confidence, and bounds should change in a discriminating way.

Read the result

Point confidence is the foreground probability exactly where you prompted. Mean confidence averages only pixels above your threshold. Coverage is the selected fraction of the image. The bounds expose the smallest rectangle containing those selected pixels.

Try centre, then top-left. A useful model must produce materially different masks rather than the same decorative overlay.
