Multi-model: “what is the thing I pointed at?”

MagicTouch first converts your point into a transparent object cut-out. A second genuine local model, MobileViT-Small q8, classifies only those selected pixels. The two pinned runtimes have separate download consent, cache controls, workers, lifecycle, timings, and visible failure states.

Why compose them?

Whole-image classification often names the scene or the most visually dominant thing. Promptable segmentation lets the user specify intent first. Passing the cut-out to a compact classifier turns a spatial question—“this thing here”—into a focused recognition input. Try the cat, then move the point to its background: both the mask statistics and labels should change honestly.

MobileViT predicts ImageNet-1k classes, so it can be specific or surprising; its top-five scores are shown, never rewritten into a more convenient answer.
