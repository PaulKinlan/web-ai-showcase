Practical: triage a reported-phish queue

The everyday job of a security-ops analyst: work through the messages employees reported as suspicious and decide which ones are real attacks. Paste one message per line, hit triage, and watch the board fill in — each message scored on-device and dropped into Threat or Clear with its confidence, so analysts start with the highest-confidence catches.

Each row lands the moment the worker finishes scoring it, so a big paste fills the board progressively rather than freezing the page. Nothing is uploaded — the whole queue is classified in a Web Worker on your device, which matters when the messages themselves are evidence.
