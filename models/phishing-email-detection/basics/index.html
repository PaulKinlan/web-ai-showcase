Basics: phishing or legitimate, live

The simplest thing this model does: read an email or URL and decide whether it looks like a phishing lure or a legitimate message, with a confidence for each. Type in the box and the verdict updates a moment after you pause. Swap "verify your password" for "lunch tomorrow" and watch the meter swing.

The model was trained on phishing emails and URLs, so it is confident on classic lures (urgency, credential verification, look-alike domains) and on plain conversational messages — and wobblier on short, ambiguous, or off-domain text. Check the confidence, not just the label.
