Wild: the story remix machine

A language model isn't one writer — it's a distribution of writers. The same opener at temperature 0.3 is safe and tidy; at 1.6 it gets gloriously weird. Generate three remixes of one story and feel what "sampling" really does, live, on a 15MB model that responds instantly.

backend – temperature – new tokens – total time – tok/s –

Low temperature picks the most probable next tokens — safe but repetitive. High temperature rolls the dice wider — surprising, sometimes nonsense. Every remix is a real generation; hit "another version" as many times as you like.
