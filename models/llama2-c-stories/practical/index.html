Practical: a bedtime-story factory

The model was trained on stories with a consistent shape: a character, a small problem, a solution, a moral. Give it those three ingredients and it drafts a clean, short children's story — the kind of private, offline, no-tracking generator a parenting app or classroom tool could actually ship.

The opener is assembled from your choices in TinyStories style; the model writes the rest. Keep it short — TinyStories models shine at ~100 tokens, and the moral usually lands in the last sentence.
