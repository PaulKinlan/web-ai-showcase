Wild: live webcam OCR

Point your camera at some text, line it up inside the reticle, and read it. The frame never leaves your device — the camera feeds straight into the base model, which copes with real-world blur and contrast better than its small sibling. Great for grabbing a serial number, a code, or a label off a real object.

Fit one line of text between the guide lines, then read. Camera access is only requested when you press Start, and stops when you leave the page.
