What you're seeing

Demucs is an 80M-parameter hybrid transformer trained to unmix music. Here it runs on the CPU (WASM) inside a Web Worker — about ten seconds for the model's fixed ~7.8-second window. Each card below is one of the model's four real output waveforms under the microscope: its measured energy (RMS, peak, share of the window), the exact window timing, and a plain statement of what the model was trained to assign to that output. Press a stem's play button to hear it alone, download it as a WAV, or “All stems” to hear them sum back into the song. Nothing is uploaded.
