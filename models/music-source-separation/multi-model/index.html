Multi-model · Demucs → Whisper ASR · WASM

One model isolates the vocal. Another tries to read the lyrics.

A real two-model chain: Hybrid Transformer Demucs separates the song and hands the vocals stem to a small browser Whisper (whisper-tiny.en), which transcribes whatever it hears. The transcript is real and editable — and so is the verdict: for this song Whisper hears no words at all, and the page shows you exactly why, with measurements.

Example 2 — a specialist cross-examines the stems

A second opinion from a second model: the 9-class instrument specialist (onnx-community/Musical-Instrument-Classification-ONNX, audio-classification, ~95 MB q8) listens to each separated stem and says which instrument family it hears. It has no voice class — its take on the vocals stem is N/A by design, and inconclusive verdicts are shown honestly, not hidden. Explicit download only.

What you're seeing

Everything above runs live in this tab: Demucs (an 80M-parameter hybrid transformer) separates the opening window into four stems, the vocals stem is resampled to 16 kHz mono, and a Whisper tiny English worker transcribes it — then, as a control, the same worker transcribes the original mix. Both models load only when YOU press their Download buttons, and the timings shown are the measured wall-clock figures from this run. The chain itself is proven healthy: the same worker transcribes spoken word cleanly. For this song it hears nothing — a small speech-trained ASR model is defeated by melodic singing, and the ONNX export's vocal stem is lossier than the reference PyTorch model on top of that. We show the empty transcript rather than invent lyrics.
