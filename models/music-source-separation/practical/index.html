What you're seeing

Every download is rendered in-page from the model's actual separated waveforms — the karaoke mix is the literal sum of the three non-vocal stems, so what you download is exactly what the model produced. The compare player rides the same real stems through one GainNode per stem: switching between Backing, Vocal and Full mix flips those gains live, so you hear the difference instantly on one timeline. Demucs separates its fixed ~7.8-second window per run (the first verse for the sample, the start of your own file), so playback loops and exports are clips, not whole songs.
