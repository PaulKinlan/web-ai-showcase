Be the DJ: remix the stems live

Demucs separates the sample song into four stems — then the page loops them through one live gain node per stem. Ride the faders and pads while it plays: drop the vocals for an instant karaoke break, boost the drums for the club, solo the bass — then download your recombined mix as a real WAV. No re-render, no waiting — the mix changes as you move a control.

What you're seeing

This is the same four real stem waveforms as the other demos, but instead of bouncing a mix the page routes each stem through its own WebAudio GainNode and loops them in sync. The pads mute, the faders ride the gains continuously (0–150%, 20 ms ramps, no clicks), and the presets just set both at once — measurably. “Download this mix” renders the current pad + fader positions to a real stereo WAV with the same gain maths. Nothing here is decorative: every control moves a real gain on the model's actual stems.
