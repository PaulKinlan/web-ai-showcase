Wild: finish the proverb

"A penny saved is a penny…" — RoBERTa read ~160 GB of English, so how many sayings did it memorise? We mask the final word of twelve proverbs and idioms, run them all in one batch, and show the top-3 completions with their real probabilities. Certainty is earned; hedging is data.

Every card is a real forward pass over the same model — twelve prompts, one worker, no canned answers. A “miss” is often the more interesting result: the model tells you which wording it actually saw most.
