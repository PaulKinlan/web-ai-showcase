Practical: which word fits better?

Torn between "great" and "excellent"? "happy" or "thrilled"? Put a <mask> in your sentence, list the candidates, and RoBERTa scores each one with the real logit it assigns in your context — a private, offline second opinion on word choice.

Each bar is the candidate's probability at the mask, softmaxed against the whole 50,265-token vocabulary — so common words start with an advantage and context does the rest. Multi-word candidates are compared by their leading subword (a deliberate simplification).
