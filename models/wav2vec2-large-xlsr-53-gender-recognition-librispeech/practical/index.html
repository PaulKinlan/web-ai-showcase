Practical — within-utterance segment stability audit

A deliberately non-operational MODEL-RISK exercise: run one approved recording as a whole and as four disclosed segments, then measure how much the checkpoint's two acoustic-label scores vary.

Limits are part of the result

This page accepts no expected “female”/“male” record, identifies no speaker, compares no inferred label to identity metadata, flags no person, and exports no person-level classification. It reports only measured score sensitivity/variation within this one recording. Segment content and heavy zero-padding change together, so variation cannot be attributed to identity or to any one acoustic feature.

How to interpret it

  • Probability delta: the observed change in one configured label's softmax score relative to the whole-utterance baseline.
  • Top-label crossing: “yes” only when the highest-scoring configured label actually differs from baseline; score movement alone is not a crossing.
  • Not confidence: softmax scores and their gap are uncalibrated; this exercise measures output sensitivity, not correctness.
  • Not deployable QA: the exercise intentionally has no identity metadata input, mismatch decision, review queue, or export.
