Wild — conditional playback-rate resampling probe

Resample one approved recording at seven playback rates, then measure the observed probability deltas and whether the highest-scoring configured label actually crosses. Every point is a genuine worker inference.

What this transformation cannot prove

Playback-rate resampling changes pitch, spectrum/formants, tempo, duration, and temporal structure together. It isolates neither pitch nor timbre, and it proves nothing about a reader's identity. A probability delta only shows conditional sensitivity to this compound transformation for this recording. A top-label crossing is reported only when measured.

Run the probe

The 4.91 s LibriSpeech recording is resampled at 0.70×–1.40×. Each variant is then deterministically cropped or zero-padded to exactly 5.000 s at 16 kHz mono, following the model card. Slow variants are cropped; fast variants are padded.

Reading the result

  • Movement is conditional: the chart truthfully shows the observed softmax probability at each rate; it does not assume monotonic movement.
  • Crossing is Boolean: “yes” means the top configured label differs from the 1.00× baseline; otherwise it says “no”, even when probabilities changed.
  • Compound intervention: because many acoustic and temporal properties change together, no result identifies a causal feature.
  • Identity limit: neither stability nor a crossing says anything about who the reader is.
