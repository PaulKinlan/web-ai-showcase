Practical: batch-translate Dutch strings to English

A Dutch-facing product generates Dutch text your international team can't read: reviews, support tickets, UI strings from the nl locale. Paste them (one per line) and get a first-pass English translation of the whole batch from one small model, on-device — a strong draft to hand a reviewer, with no server and no per-string fee.

strings –pair –backend –total time –

Machine translation is a strong first draft, not a substitute for a native speaker — but getting the whole string table translated in seconds, offline, is a real head start. The default strings are a Dutch locale file; replace them with your own reviews or tickets.
