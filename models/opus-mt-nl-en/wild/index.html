Wild: round-trip drift

Take a Dutch sentence, translate it to English, then translate the result straight back to Dutch with the reverse OPUS-MT model (English → Dutch, downloaded on demand). If the meaning survived the trip, you come home close to where you started; if it drifted, you can see exactly where. Two small models, out and back — a fidelity check you can watch.

similarity –backend –total time –

Idioms and irony drift fastest — they lean on meanings that don't survive a literal hop. Plain, concrete sentences round-trip cleanly. A round-trip that comes back different doesn't prove the forward translation is wrong, but it's exactly where a human reviewer should look first.
