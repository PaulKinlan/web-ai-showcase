Practical: digitise a handwritten note

The workflow a business actually ships: snap a photo of a handwritten note — a meeting page, a filled form, an exercise book — and turn it into editable, copyable text. No OCR API, no per-page bill, and the note never leaves the device.

Why line-by-line? TrOCR is a single-line reader — you choose the reading order and skip the margins, doodles, and printed prompts a full-page OCR would choke on. Each row is editable before you copy: the model proposes, you correct.
