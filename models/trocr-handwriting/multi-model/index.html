Multi-model: read handwriting → translate

One model can't do this alone. First TrOCR (handwritten) reads the pen strokes into text; then M2M100 translates that text into the language you pick. Ink → characters → another language, chained end-to-end, all on your device.

Why chain them? Handwriting OCR frees text from ink; translation makes it readable to anyone. Together they're a private, offline "write-and-share-across-languages" tool — no image and no text ever leaves the device. (TrOCR small-handwritten reads English handwriting, so stage one transcribes English and stage two translates outward.)
