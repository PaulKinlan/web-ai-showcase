Wild: instruction roulette

A 125M model trained on two million instructions will answer almost anything with total seriousness. Spin the wheel: explain a conspiracy theory as a recipe, write a job description for a cat, describe an elephant in pirate speak. Every answer is a real generation, streamed live.
