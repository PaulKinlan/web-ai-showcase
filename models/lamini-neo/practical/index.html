Practical: the rewriter's desk

A genuinely useful pattern for a small instruction model: give it one sentence and three rewrite briefs — more formal, shorter, friendlier — and it produces three versions, entirely on-device. The kind of private copy-drafting an email or notes tool could ship.

backend – new tokens – total time –

The instruction is assembled as ### Instruction: {brief} "{sentence}". Results are drafts — review them like any generated copy. Keep sentences short: a 125M model shines at tight rewrites.
